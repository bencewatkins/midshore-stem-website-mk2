Our STEM camp is created specifically for curious young kids that want to learn more, turning difficult concepts into hands on small adventures. We believe that you’re never too young to start exploring. So we break down science, technology, engineering, and math through interactive play, exciting experiments, and collaborative projects. By cultivating curiosity and making learning feel easier and more fun to do, we help kids truly understand how the world works, inspiring the next generation of thinkers, creators, and problem-solvers one breakthrough at a time. Camps usually last four summer days, utilizing partnerships with local YMCA chapters and the Easton Multicultural Center. The camp is offered at no additional cost to parents.
